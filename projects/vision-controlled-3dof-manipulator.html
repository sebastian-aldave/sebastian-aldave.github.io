3-DOF manipulator controlled by computer vision

A 3-DOF robotic manipulator guided by computer vision: a camera perceives the target and the arm is controlled from its position.

  • Computer vision
  • OpenCV
  • Kinematics
  • Control

What I did

  • Detected the target with a camera using computer vision.
  • Computed the joint motion of the 3-DOF arm with inverse kinematics.
