Gesture-to-speech communication glove

Academic project · Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería, Lima · ABET project exhibition · Sep – Dec 2023

A glove that recognizes hand gestures in real time and turns them into text and synthesized speech.

  • TensorFlow
  • IoT
  • Sensors
  • ESP32

What I did

  • Developed a neural network model (TensorFlow) for real-time gesture recognition.
  • Integrated inertial and flex sensors to analyze movements, with haptic and visual feedback.
  • Deployed an IoT architecture linking the glove to a web interface for transcription and speech synthesis.
