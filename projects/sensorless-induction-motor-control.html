Robust sensorless induction motor control

Research project · Mines Paris–PSL, Centre d'Automatique et Systèmes · Dec 2024 – Feb 2025

  • Nonlinear control
  • Kalman filter
  • MATLAB/Simulink

Project description

Control of an induction motor without a speed sensor, designed to stay stable under load disturbances and uncertain parameters.

  • Synthesized nonlinear control laws for flux-speed control of an induction motor.
  • Designed an observer based on cascaded Kalman filters to estimate speed without a sensor.
  • Analyzed stability and validated the design in simulation under load disturbances and parametric uncertainties.
